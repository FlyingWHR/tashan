— THE NUMBERS

The MCP ecosystem, measured.

We track 39,374 MCP servers, agent skills and plugins. 14,419 carry a score, 7,862 have been scanned for advisories at the version you would install today, and 74% of those ship with no build provenance.

Every figure on this page is a count of rows, recomputed nightly and derived from public evidence. Nothing here is behind the paywall — a fact you have to pay for is a fact nobody repeats. How we measure · What changed recently

Coverage, weighted by demand

The aggregate ratio falls every time discovery succeeds — finding a thousand new capabilities makes the fraction we have measured smaller while nothing has got worse. So it is reported and never targeted. What we commit to is the demand curve, because what costs a reader is an absence on the thing they looked up, not a gap in the tail.
