Turn Pro on for this machine

Your licence key is in your account and in your purchase email. You need it once per machine:

npx tashan-cli activate <your key>

npx tashan-cli doctor then prints Pro · licence active on every run, so you can see it working. Same key on every machine you use.

This site looks the same whether you pay or not. Every finding, score and grade is public — Pro adds the detail you act on, in the CLI and on capability pages. There is no members' area to look for.

If something is wrong

  • Lost your key — sign in above, or check your purchase email.
  • New machine — run activate again with the same key. One subscription covers all of them.
  • "This device was deactivated" — your subscription is fine, this machine's slot was released. Run activate again.
  • Anything else — hello@tashan.sh · cancel or refund
