Your account

There is no tashan password to make. Billing, your licence key and the cancel button all live in one place, and Pro itself lives in your terminal.

Sign in to your account ›

Sign in with the email you paid with. Polar sends a one-time code, so there is nothing to remember and nothing of yours for us to lose. Polar is our merchant of record — they take the payment, which is why the account is theirs and why we never see your card.

What is in there

  • Your licence key
  • Invoices and receipts
  • The devices you have activated, and a button to release any of them
  • Your payment method
  • Cancel, any time, without asking us

What happens after you pay

  1. Polar issues a licence key and emails it to you. It is also on the checkout success page and always in your account.
  2. Switch it on, once per machine:
    npx tashan-cli activate <your key>
  3. Run npx tashan-cli doctor. Every run prints Pro · licence active, so you never have to wonder whether it took.
The website looks the same whether you pay or not, and that is deliberate. Nothing here is hidden behind a login — every security finding, every score and every grade is public. What Pro adds is the detail you act on, and it appears in the CLI and on the capability pages you unlock. If you are looking for a members' area, there isn't one to find.

Why no tashan login

A login means storing passwords, running sessions and holding personal data — for a $6/month tool that reads public evidence and never sees your config. Polar already does the billing side properly, so adding our own account system would mean two places to sign in, two places to get locked out of, and a database of yours for us to leak. The licence key is the whole credential.

Something wrong

  • Lost the key — it is in your account, and in the receipt email.
  • New machine — run activate again with the same key. One subscription covers your machines; each registers under its own hostname.
  • doctor says the device was deactivated — your subscription is fine, this machine's slot was released. Run activate again.
  • doctor says the key is not valid — copy it again from the portal.
  • Anything else — hello@tashan.sh, or see refunds and cancellation.
