Questions

Is the data really free?

Yes, and it stays that way — every score, every capability, no key and no quota. It's how the field gets an honest reference, and a number behind a paywall is worth less to everyone including us. Pro doesn't unlock scores; it watches your stack and keeps the history.

Why isn't there a price yet?

Because nobody has used it. We'd be guessing, and a guessed price anchors the thing for years. The waitlist asks which part you'd actually pay for; that answer sets the price. Same rule as the scores — measured, not claimed.

Can I pay to rank higher?

No — and that's the whole point. There is no promoted slot and no way to buy a score. Read the methodology.

Do you sell the capabilities?

No. We sell nothing we measure, host nothing, and take no cut of anything. Every other index in this field either sells the listings, hosts the servers, or earns when you run them — so a low verdict costs them money. It costs us nothing, which is the only reason our verdict is worth reading (see About).
