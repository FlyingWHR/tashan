Questions

Is the data really free?

Yes, and it stays that way — every score, every capability, no key and no quota. It's how the field gets an honest reference, and a number behind a paywall is worth less to everyone including us. Pro doesn't unlock scores; it watches your stack and keeps the history.

What am I actually paying for today?

tashan doctor --trend, and the score history that makes it possible. One row in the list above is marked not built yet and you are not paying for it — it's there so you can see where Pro is going, and it arrives at this price. We'd rather show you an honest short list than sell a long one.

Why isn't this free like everything else?

Because it's the one thing that can't be re-derived from today's public data. Every score on the Index is a snapshot anyone could recompute; the series only exists because we recorded it, every day, and a day we miss is gone for good — for us too. That's what $6 buys, and it's worth more every month it runs.

How do I use the key?

export TASHAN_KEY=… then tashan doctor --trend. The key is in your Polar portal. Nothing about your config is uploaded — the CLI reads your local files and asks us only about capability names. Full details on support.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — 14 days, no reason needed, and after that we'll refund the unused period if Pro was broken or not what this page described. Cancel yourself any time in the portal. Full refund policy.

Can I pay to rank higher?

No — and that's the whole point. There is no promoted slot and no way to buy a score. Read the methodology.

Do you sell the capabilities?

No. We sell nothing we measure, host nothing, and take no cut of anything. Every other index in this field either sells the listings, hosts the servers, or earns when you run them — so a low verdict costs them money. It costs us nothing, which is the only reason our verdict is worth reading (see About).
