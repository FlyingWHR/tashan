# Music Catalog Diligence

> Music Catalog Diligence helps catalog buyers, sellers, and financing teams review music catalog deals faster and with better evidence. It turns messy data rooms into source-cited workpapers by normalizing royalty statements, checking rights and metadata gaps, analyzing NPS/NLS, flagging diligence risks, and preparing buyer, seller, or lender-ready deal packages.
