# LinkedIn Poster

> Drafts and publishes high-quality LinkedIn posts that sound like the user, not like an AI summary. Reads a reusable per-user profile (interests, audience, voice samples, archetypes, forbidden phrases) and runs a per-post loop: pick an archetype (news reaction, lesson, milestone, provocative question, story), source content, draft 2-3 variants, surface for explicit approval, publish.
