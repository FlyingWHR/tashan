# Indian Family Drafting

> For every advocate practising before India's Family Courts under the Family Courts Act 1984 and the matrimonial-jurisdiction tier of District Courts, this open-source Claude Code plugin standardises drafting across India's plural personal-law regimes — Hindu Marriage Act 1955, Special Marriage Act 1954, Indian Divorce Act 1869, Muslim Personal Law, Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act 1936 — and the State Family-Court overlay applicable to the parties. The structural skeleton is uniform; personal-law
