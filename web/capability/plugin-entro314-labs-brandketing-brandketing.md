# Brandketing

> Marketing intelligence plugin that builds a living context store from product docs, customer calls, CRM data, and competitor research - then compiles structured briefs and generates copy through a 6-step strategy workflow with a built-in critic pass. Separates evidence collection, human approval, brief compilation, and copy generation into distinct steps so the model never does extraction, positioning, and writing in one jump.
