# Blueprint Trigger

> Blueprint Trigger helps product and design teams capture incoming requests clearly before anyone opens Figma. It acts as a structured intake interviewer — asking the right questions, detecting ambiguous or mixed requests, and producing a ready-to-use Trigger card. This is Step 1 of the Blueprint Hub methodology, designed for teams working under pressure, especially when the Design System is still being built. The skill adapts automatically to three roles: Designer, Product Manager, and Function
