Bridge Ds

plugin

AI-powered design generation in Figma using your real design system. Bridge extracts your DS (components, tokens, styles), writes validated specs, and generates production-ready Figma designs with 100% DS compliance — zero hardcoded values, real component instances, bound variables. Features a unique learning loop that captures your corrections and improves future generations. Supports both figma-console-mcp and the official Figma MCP server.
