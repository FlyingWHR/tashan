# D2c

> Turn Figma frames into production-ready code that uses your existing components, your design tokens, and your framework conventions. d2c scans your codebase, builds a design contract, then generates, screenshots, pixel-diffs, and iterates until the output matches the design. Four skills — init, build, audit, guard — cover the full lifecycle from token extraction to ongoing drift enforcement. Supports React, Vue, Svelte, Angular, Solid, and Astro.
