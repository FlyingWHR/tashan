# Rubber Duck

> rubber-duck is a Socratic thinking partner for Claude Code. Inspired by the classic rubber duck debugging technique—but with a duck that actually talks back—it helps you think through problems instead of just handing you answers. While most AI tools focus on writing code for you, rubber-duck is designed to help you learn it. Rather than giving you the fix, it asks the right question at the right time—guiding you step by step, at your own pace. Use Claude when you need to ship fast. Use rubber
