# Portfolio Review

> Get the kind of portfolio feedback you’d actually get in a hiring room, but rarely hear. This skill breaks down your design portfolio across storytelling, structure, craft, and product thinking. It flags what’s working, what’s hurting you, and what’s quietly disqualifying you. Every review includes severity-coded feedback (from minor polish to hiring blockers), plus a clear hiring signal and level calibration. If your portfolio isn’t landing interviews, this will tell you why and what to fix
