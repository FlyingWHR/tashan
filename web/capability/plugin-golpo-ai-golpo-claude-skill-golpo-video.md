# Golpo Video

> Generate beautiful explainer videos with Golpo from prompts, scripts, audio, or documents. The Golpo skill turns a Claude Code conversation into a finished AI video without leaving your editor. Describe what you want — a topic, a custom script, an audio narration, or a PDF/DOCX to summarize — and Claude submits the job to Golpo's video API, polls until rendering completes, and auto-downloads the MP4 to ~/Golpo/videos/ with a readable filename. Supports both Golpo Sketch and Golpo Canvas engines,
