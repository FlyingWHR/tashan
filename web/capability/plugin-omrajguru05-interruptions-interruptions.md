# Interruptions

> An automated auditor that finds hidden edge cases in your code before they reach your users. It checks your project against 12 specific failure categories—including network drops, race conditions, and security gaps—and writes a structured report of its findings. Once you review the report, it helps you safely apply fixes to your files one at a time.
