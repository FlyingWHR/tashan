# Supreme Court Drafting

> For every advocate-on-record and senior counsel who files before the Supreme Court of India — and every junior who drafts under one — this is an open-source Claude Code plugin that takes a case folder (facts, pleadings, statutory anchor, prayer brief) and walks it through a six-agent pipeline (Reader → Format → Drafter → Verifier → Refiner → Overseer) the way a Supreme Court chamber actually works. Covers Special Leave Petitions (Civil and Criminal), Article 32 Writ Petitions, Transfer Petitions
