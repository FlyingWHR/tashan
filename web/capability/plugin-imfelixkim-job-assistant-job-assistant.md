# Job Assistant

> Analyze job postings and optimize your resume with an 11-step interactive workflow. The plugin conducts company research, structures job requirements, performs gap analysis between your resume and the posting, optimizes your resume for ATS keywords, and delivers a 4-perspective review (practitioner, recruiter, HR, CEO). Includes 8 specialized agents and 3 skills. Supports Korean job market with privacy-first design (personal info masking throughout the process).
