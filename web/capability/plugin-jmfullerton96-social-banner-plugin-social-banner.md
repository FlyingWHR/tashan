# Social Banner

> Generate professional banner and header images for social media profiles. Supports LinkedIn, X/Twitter, YouTube, and Facebook with a design-first workflow. Claude gathers your design direction, generates a draft using Pillow, iterates on feedback, then exports at the correct dimensions for each platform. Handles text banners (name + tagline), text-free artistic banners, and freeform design specs. Produces visually striking results that avoid generic AI aesthetics.
