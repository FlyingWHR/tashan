# Legalrabbit Docx

> LegalRabbit DOCX plugin enables your Cowork to create, modify, redline, and comment on your docx files. We've built the DOCX engine from the ground up to work well with AI. It consumes much fewer tokens and runs much faster (compared to the alternatives). The plugin also works offline, ensuring data privacy and security of your data.
