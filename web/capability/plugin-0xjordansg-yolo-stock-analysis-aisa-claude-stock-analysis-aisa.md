# Stock Analysis Aisa

> AI-powered stock and crypto analysis plugin powered by AIsa API. Features include: 8-dimension scoring with BUY/HOLD/SELL signals, dividend analysis with safety scores, portfolio management with live P&L tracking, watchlist with price alerts, Hot Scanner for trending stocks and crypto, and Rumor Scanner for M&A rumors, insider activity, and analyst signals. Supports both US stocks and major cryptocurrencies via Yahoo Finance data.
