# Privacypilot

> Automatically generate Play Store-compliant privacy policies for Android apps. Scans your AndroidManifest.xml to detect every permission and your build.gradle to detect third-party SDKs (AdMob, Firebase, Crashlytics, etc.), then generates a complete HTML privacy policy covering GDPR, CCPA, COPPA, and Google Play Data Safety requirements. Deploys directly to GitHub Pages.
