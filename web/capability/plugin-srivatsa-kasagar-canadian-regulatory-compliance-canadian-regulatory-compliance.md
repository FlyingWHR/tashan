# Canadian Regulatory Compliance

> A Claude Code skill that answers Canadian compliance questions across 13 regulatory domains — privacy, employment, CASL, tax, and more. Canadian regulatory law is complex, spans multiple jurisdictions, and changes often. This plugin gives your team instant access to practical guidance across eleven regulatory areas, helping you understand your obligations, review documents, generate checklists, draft notices, and get quick answers — all without having to wade through dense legislation.
