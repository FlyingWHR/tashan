# Csitrep Generator

> Multi-agent Situation Report (SitRep) generator for any project type. Drops 25 specialist AI agents across 5 domains to read your project documents in parallel and synthesizes findings into a structured status report — with critical issues, watch items, and recommended actions classified by urgency. Supports six project types out of the box: Construction (schedule, cost, safety, contracts, site), Software/IT (sprint, budget, quality, blockers, infra), Development Programs (workplan, budget/grant
