# Truehue

> True Hue runs a protocol modeled on what a certified color analyst runs in a $300–$400 in-person consultation. That means five calibrated photos, a 15+ item questionnaire, and four-dimension analysis (undertone, value, chroma, contrast) with evidence cited from specific photos and answers. Before a season is named, a mandatory mid-analysis check-in catches misreads. After draping comparison against both adjacent seasons, you get the full deliverable: ~36 hex codes by wardrobe role, colors to avo
