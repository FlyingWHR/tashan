# Judicial Separation Draft

> Draft a petition for judicial separation under Section 10 of the Hindu Marriage Act 1955 / Section 23 of the Special Marriage Act 1954 / Section 22 of the Indian Divorce Act 1869 / equivalent personal-law provisions. Judicial separation suspends the obligation of cohabitation without dissolving the marriage and is often filed (a) as a precursor to divorce on the ground of non-resumption-of-cohabitation, or (b) where the petitioner is religiously or personally averse to dissolution but seeks formal separation. Personal law is sourced from family-config.md. Produces complete petition with marriage particulars, grievance narrative, grounds (same grounds as divorce under each personal law), prayer, verification, supporting affidavit, list of documents, and interim-maintenance application. Auto-fires on "draft judicial separation", "draft JS petition", and similar trigger phrases.
