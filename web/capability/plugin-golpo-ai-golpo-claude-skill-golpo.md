# Golpo

> Generate AI explainer videos from prompts, scripts, audio files, or documents. Golpo creates animated explainer videos, podcast-to-video content, and script-to-video productions. Supports multiple visual styles (Sketch Classic and Canvas), 44+ languages, various voices, and background music. Auto-downloads rendered MP4 files locally. Perfect for educational content, marketing videos, and social media shorts.
