# Fluidityiq Patent Intelligence

> Patent Intelligence automates patentability assessments for IP teams. It connects to the FluidityIQ Patents MCP to perform semantic prior art searches across global patent databases, classify references by threat level (anticipatory, combinable, or background), build feature-to-prior-art coverage matrices with specific citations, and generate structured Patentability Assessment Reports with novelty and non-obviousness analysis.
