# Universal Design Principles

> A working library of 42 design principles drawn from Universal Principles of Design (Lidwell, Holden, Butler) and the broader HCI research literature. Install one or more of the five composable plugins and your Claude agent gains 137 curated skills that fire automatically during any UX, UI, or product design task. The principles apply across design systems, frameworks, and platforms.
