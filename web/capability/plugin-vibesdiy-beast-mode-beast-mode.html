beast-mode

plugin:vibesdiy/beast-mode/beast-mode · plugin

Follow-through for high-fan-out development. Optimized for the workflow where a human fires off many parallel, well-scoped work streams — the risk isn't the changes, it's forgetting to finish them. beast-mode is the discipline that makes every fired stream land: it always ends in a PR, absorbs review autonomously, arms the merge on green, closes the issues it fixes, hands off what the environment can't do as a runbook issue, and captures what it learned.
