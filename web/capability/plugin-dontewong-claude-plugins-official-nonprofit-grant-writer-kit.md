# Nonprofit Grant Writer Kit

> Generate foundation-ready grant documents for nonprofits. Five production-ready generators covering Grant Narrative, Logic Model, Program Budget Narrative, Letter of Inquiry, and Funder Alignment Assessment. Built around standard funder formats so nonprofits can apply to more grants in less time. Built for executive directors, development staff, and grant writers at small-to-mid nonprofits who need to scale their grant pipeline without scaling their team.
