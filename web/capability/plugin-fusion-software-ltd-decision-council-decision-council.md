# Decision Council

> AI advisory panel for decisions that matter. Decision Council assembles a panel of independent AI advisors to debate your decision from multiple angles, cross-examine each other's reasoning, and deliver a scored recommendation with a visual dashboard. Includes a brutal-truth Mirror agent, pre-mortem analysis, and a structured decision-recording flow that builds your decision track record over time.
