# Prompt Enhancer Pack

> Auto-enhances vague prompts into well-structured, context-aware prompts before Claude responds. Includes four skills: /enhance (general), /enhance-code (detects your tech stack from package.json, requirements.txt, etc. and tailors the prompt), /enhance-writing (classifies into 6 writing types and applies the right framework), and /enhance-for (rewrites for ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, or Copilot).
