# Competition Checker

> Reviews startup competition T&Cs and produces a neutral, evidence-first scorecard across 7 founder-calibrated dimensions: IP & ownership, equity & financial, exclusivity & restraint, publicity & data, commitment cost, jurisdiction & disputes, and prize mechanics. Every flag quotes the clause verbatim with a source reference. Flags what T&Cs say AND what they don't say (omission detection). Investment-term deep dive for SAFE/convertible-note prizes. Cross-competition side-by-side comparison. Opti
