# Signal Hunter

> Daily AI-powered trend hunter that aggregates content from 15+ sources — HackerNews, GitHub Trending, Reddit, YouTube (with transcripts), X/Twitter, Product Hunt, HuggingFace Papers, Gmail newsletters, and app stores. Detects cross-source trending signals by finding topics appearing in 3+ sources simultaneously, rates them by strength, and generates structured Obsidian notes with business ideas (apps, SaaS, BaaS, gaming).
