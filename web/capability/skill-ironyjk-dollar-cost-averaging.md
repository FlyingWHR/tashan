# Dollar Cost Averaging

> Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) — buy at fixed intervals with fixed amounts. Diversifies timing-failure risk and controls behavioral bias. Theoretically has lower expected value than Lump-Sum, but offers major advantages in emotional stability and executability.
