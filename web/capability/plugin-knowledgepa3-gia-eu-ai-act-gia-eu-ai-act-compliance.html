Gia Eu AI Act Compliance

plugin

Classify any AI system under the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689). Screens all 8 prohibited practices (Article 5), classifies high-risk systems across all 8 Annex III categories, calculates penalty exposure (up to 7% global turnover), and generates structured compliance reports. Built for compliance officers, CISOs, DPOs, and legal teams preparing for the August 2, 2026 enforcement deadline. Powered by GIA (Governance Intelligence Automation) — the open governance framework for AI agents.
