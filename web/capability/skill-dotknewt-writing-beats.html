Writing Beats · dotknewt

skill

Use when the user has raw material — a fragments doc, notes, a transcript, a pile of research — and wants it drafted into a long-form article or post one beat at a time, picking a path through it interactively instead of getting a full draft dumped at once. Triggers on requests like "turn this into an article", "shape these notes into a post, beat by beat", "let's draft this journey-style, choose-your-own-adventure", or "give me a few directions to pick from and write one at a time". Also covers continuing or revising an in-progress beats article, including "go back and rewrite beat 3" or "try a different beat 2". …
