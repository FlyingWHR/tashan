# Cyclebottom Risk

> Real-time risk levels for crypto and stock indices from CycleBottom. Returns a normalized 0-1 risk score based on logarithmic deviation from the 365-day moving average for BTC, ETH, ADA, SOL, BNB, LINK, Dow Jones, S&P 500, and NASDAQ. Low risk means historically cheap (accumulation zone), high risk means historically overvalued (distribution zone). Just ask "What's the BTC risk?" or "Is the S&P 500 overvalued?" and get instant data.
