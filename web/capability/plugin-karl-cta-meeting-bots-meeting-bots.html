meeting-bots

plugin:karl-cta/meeting-bots/meeting-bots · plugin

A panel of AI personas that debate your decisions inside Claude Code. 3 to 10 participants across 5 teams (dev, design, product, business, life), 3 rounds of parallel debate, and a user-centric synthesis from the Boss. The full debate is recorded to a markdown file while the console stays clean.

Works with: Claude Code, Cursor, Claude Desktop, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, Cline, Windsurf, VS Code
