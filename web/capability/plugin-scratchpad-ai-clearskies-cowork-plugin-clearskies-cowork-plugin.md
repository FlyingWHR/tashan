# Clearskies Cowork Plugin

> Clearskies connects Salesforce, Gong, Scratchpad, Google, Microsoft, and Slack into a Customer Context Graph — a live, unified view of every account — then exposes it to Claude via MCP. The Cowork plugin puts that on top of six native GTM workflows: account briefs, deal risk scoring, call prep, post-call summaries, pipeline review, and personalized outreach. Works standalone; supercharged when connected to your GTM stack.
