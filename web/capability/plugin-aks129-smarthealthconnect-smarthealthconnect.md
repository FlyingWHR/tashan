# Smarthealthconnect

> SmartHealthConnect turns Claude into a patient-facing health copilot. Six auto-discoverable skills invoke themselves based on natural prompts: • care-completion — tracks preventive screenings, referrals, and follow-ups against HEDIS and USPSTF quality measures. Triggers on "what screenings am I due for", "show my care gaps". • medication-refills — monitors refill windows, requests refills, and projects 90-day timelines. Triggers on "refill my lisinopril", "which meds am I about to run out of".
