# Deepmiro

> Predict how people and communities will react to events, policies, announcements, or documents — before they happen. DeepMiro creates hundreds of AI personas (journalists, critics, supporters, officials, regulators) and lets them debate your scenario on simulated Twitter and Reddit platforms. You get back a structured prediction report, can interview any persona about their reasoning, and upload documents (PDFs, reports, regulations) to ground predictions. DeepMiro runs a full prediction in 7 mi
