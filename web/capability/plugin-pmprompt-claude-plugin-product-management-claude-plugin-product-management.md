# Claude Plugin Product Management

> A product management plugin for Claude that helps you draft clear, shippable product docs faster. It generates PRDs, user stories with acceptance criteria, stakeholder updates, and prioritization frameworks (RICE, competitive analysis, positioning) with structured outputs and practical checklists. Use it to turn messy inputs into crisp specs, align cross‑functional teams, and keep your thinking consistent across artifacts.
