# Skill Campfire

> Turn your Cowork skills into living characters who hang out around a virtual campfire. Every installed skill becomes a character with a personality, voice, opinions, and relationships with other skills. They talk to each other, joke, argue, and connect — while you sit back and enjoy. Zero config.
