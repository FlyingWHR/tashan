# Pinterest Marketing Strategist

> Produce composed Pinterest pins from your real documents and approved image library. Reads your Google Drive folder for brand context, scans your image library for visual assets, matches images to generated copy, and outputs pins ready to review and post. Tracks performance and learns from every batch — better images, better copy, better boards, every cycle.
