aria

plugin:patchline-ai/aria/aria · plugin

Aria is a guided music-release workflow for artists, managers, and labels. It walks through a complete lifecycle — from raw idea or finished track to pitched, released, and live on a smart link — through nine progressive phase skills and the hosted aria MCP. Every artifact is grounded in your real catalog, streaming intelligence, and sonic analysis (BPM, key, genres, moods). No hallucinated playlists. No invented metrics.
