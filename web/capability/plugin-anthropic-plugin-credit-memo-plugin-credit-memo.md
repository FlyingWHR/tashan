# Credit Memo

> Generate institutional-grade Credit Memorandums in minutes. Automatically collects 3–5 years of financial data from SEC EDGAR, SerpApi, and major rating agencies. Outputs branded Word, PDF, and PowerPoint with 10 embedded charts, inline citations, and a 5-layer compliance guardrails system. Supports public and private companies.
