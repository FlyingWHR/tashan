# Decision Simulator

> Decision Simulator improves Claude Code’s judgment at technical decision points. It helps Claude compare real tradeoffs, surface hidden costs, detect missing context, and make grounded recommendations when choosing between implementation paths, refactors, libraries, boundaries, and architecture decisions. It is designed to improve both user-facing answers and Claude’s own mid-task decision-making without taking over the workflow.
