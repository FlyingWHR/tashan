# Smb Hr Compliance Pack

> Generate state-compliant HR documents for small and mid-size businesses. Five production-ready generators covering Offer Letter, Employee Handbook, Performance Improvement Plan, Termination Package (checklist + letter + paycheck guide), and 90-Day Onboarding Checklist. State-aware language (at-will, final-paycheck rules, leave laws) reduces legal risk for SMBs without an in-house HR team. Built for founders, office managers, and HR consultants who need defensible employment documents in minutes
