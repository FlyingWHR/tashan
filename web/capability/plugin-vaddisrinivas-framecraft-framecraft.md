# Framecraft

> Generate polished demo videos from a single prompt. The plugin teaches your LLM story structure, visual design, and narration craft, then orchestrates Playwright, FFmpeg, and Edge TTS MCP servers to render 1920x1080 videos with voiceover and transitions. No screen recording, no editing — describe your product and get a video.
