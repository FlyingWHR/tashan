Jobpilot

plugin

Automate your job search end-to-end with Claude Code. JobPilot searches job boards, scores matches against your resume, auto-fills application forms via browser automation, generates tailored cover letters and Upwork proposals, and preps you for interviews. The Autopilot skill lets you approve a batch of jobs once, then walk away while Claude applies to all of them - with progress tracking and resumability.
