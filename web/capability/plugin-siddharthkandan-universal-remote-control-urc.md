# Urc

> Control Codex and Gemini from the Claude app on your phone. URC spawns a lightweight Haiku relay that bridges your phone's Remote Control session to any Codex or Gemini pane running in tmux. One app, three AI ecosystems. Also includes a cross-CLI teams protocol for structured agent-to-agent communication via MCP.
