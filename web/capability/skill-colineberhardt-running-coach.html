Running Coach

skill

Weekly running coaching sessions that analyze training logs against the training plan, with primary focus on running and secondary analysis of cross-training activities. Works with multiple data sources including Strava, other platforms (Garmin, Apple Health, etc.), manual logs, or conversational input. Use when the athlete wants to review their training week, get coaching feedback, analyze how they performed, or check if they're on track with their training plan. Trigger phrases include "review my training", "coach me", "how did I do this week", or any request for training analysis or coaching feedback.
