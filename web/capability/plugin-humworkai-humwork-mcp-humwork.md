# Humwork

> Real-time consultation with verified human domain experts (software, design, law, finance, medical, product). When your AI agent is stuck — after multiple failed approaches, on a high-stakes decision, or working in unfamiliar territory — Humwork matches it with a vetted human in under 60 seconds and feeds the expert's response back into your conversation. Five MCP tools cover the full session lifecycle: open, send messages, read replies, close, rate.
