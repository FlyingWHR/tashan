Orchestrate · scdenney

skill

Orchestrate complex work from an active gpt-6-astra or gpt-5.6-sol Codex session. The detected lead owns decomposition, integration, and verification; Astra keeps the hardest reasoning in the lead, while Sol escalates unusually difficult units to Astra. Both route bounded work to cheaper GPT-5.6 tiers and can use a cross-vendor Claude peer. Not for routine single-context work. Use when the user explicitly asks to orchestrate, delegate, fan out, parallelize, assign subagents, obtain independent checks, or have Codex act as tech lead.
