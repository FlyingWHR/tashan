# Cost Estimate

> Scan any codebase (GitHub URL or local path) and calculate what a real dev team would charge to build it. Analyzes complexity across 7 factors, estimates human hours, and produces a detailed cost report for 6 hiring scenarios: Solo Dev (US), Lean Startup (US), Growth Company (US), Enterprise (US), Polish Software House, and Polish Internal Team. Includes 2025-2026 market rates from Bulldogjob, JustJoinIT, NoFluffJobs, Salary.com, and Glassdoor. Shows AI-assisted development ROI with speed
