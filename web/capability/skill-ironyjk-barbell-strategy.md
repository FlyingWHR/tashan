# Barbell Strategy

> Barbell Strategy (Nassim Taleb, Antifragile) — 85–90% in safe assets + 10–15% in high-risk, high-convexity bets, avoiding the middle. A structure that sidesteps tail risk while capturing upside optionality. Especially suited for environments of extreme uncertainty.
