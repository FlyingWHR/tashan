# Insights

> Weekly and monthly pattern recognition across Obsidian journal entries. Reads your recent journals and surfaces what you might be missing: floor trends, recurring emotional patterns, habit streaks, and blind spots. Includes a life coach voice that pushes back on comfort zones, a therapist voice that names what you're avoiding, and optional advisory panel commentary. Companion to the daily-journal plugin.
