# Ido4shape

> A specification discovery plugin for Cowork and Claude Code. Helps product managers, founders, and tech leads figure out what to build before writing a single line of code. Unlike tools that assume you already know what to build, ido4shape starts at "tell me about your problem" and guides you through creative, non-linear conversation — reading your documents, asking the questions that matter, surfacing risks you hadn't considered, and tracking understanding across multiple sessions via a persis
