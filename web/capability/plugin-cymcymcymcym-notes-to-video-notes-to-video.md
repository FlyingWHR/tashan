# Notes To Video

> Turn lecture notes (PDF, LaTeX, markdown) into animated 3Blue1Brown-style explainer videos. Handles the full pipeline: content extraction, narration writing with cue-based audio-video sync, Manim scene generation, validation, rendering, and composition. Four TTS backends including free Edge-TTS. Cross-platform (Linux/macOS/Windows), no GPU required by default.
