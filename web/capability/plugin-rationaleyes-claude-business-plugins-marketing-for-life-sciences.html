marketing-for-life-sciences

plugin:rationaleyes/claude-business-plugins/marketing-for-life-sciences · plugin

A life sciences adaptation of the marketing plugin for pharmaceutical, biotech, and medtech teams. Extends the full general marketing toolkit with regulatory compliance (FDA/EMA), MLR review workflows, HCP engagement, KOL strategy, congress marketing, and pharma-specific KPIs. Designed primarily for Cowork, also compatible with Claude Code.

Works with: Claude Code, Cursor, Claude Desktop, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, Cline, Windsurf, VS Code
