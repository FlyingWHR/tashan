About Tashan

Tashan measures the tools people give their agents. A dossier brings together upkeep, adoption, known advisories and links to the underlying evidence.

What gets a score

Scores use public registry, npm and repository signals. 1,015 catalogued entries carry no score. The index includes 722 agent skills. A skill that only shares its parent repository’s activity has too little independent evidence to rank.

What changes over time

The record spans 114 days of measurements. The change feed records new findings. The payments report tracks USDC receipts at identified addresses on Base. Revenue never changes a ranking.
