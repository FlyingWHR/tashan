Refunds & cancellation

Last updated 29 July 2026

Cancel yourself, any time, in two clicks. If Pro didn't do what you expected, email us and we'll refund it.

Cancelling

Manage everything in the Polar customer portal — sign in with the email you subscribed with and Polar sends you a one-time code. There you can cancel, change your payment method, and download every invoice and receipt. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues to the end of the period you've paid for.

Refunds

Within 14 days of a charge, ask and we'll refund it — no reason needed. Email hello@tashan.sh from your subscription address. After 14 days we'll still refund the unused portion if Pro was broken, unavailable, or materially different from what this site described.

Refunds are issued through Polar and return to the original payment method, normally within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. Any tax charged is refunded with it. A refund ends access: the licence key stops validating.

Polar may refund before we do. As merchant of record, Polar can issue a refund and cancel the subscription on its own within 60 days of a purchase in order to prevent a chargeback. We're telling you this because it means you may see a refund we did not initiate — it is not an error, and nothing on your side is required.

Please don't chargeback first

A disputed charge costs us a fixed fee whatever the outcome, and it's slower for you than an email. We have never refused a refund request. Ask first.

The free tier is unaffected

Everything free stays free whether you subscribe, cancel or are refunded — the whole Index, every score, the badges, the agent endpoints and the CLI. There is nothing to lose access to except history and the paid API.
