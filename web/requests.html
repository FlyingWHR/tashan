The demand board

Request a grade, not a placement

Other stores let you pay for a placement. Here you ask for a capability to be tracked and graded, and the grade you get is the one the evidence gives, whatever it says.

Nothing is for sale on this page. Requesting is a public GitHub issue, so the demand is itself public evidence — no account, no payment, and no queue you can buy your way up. If paid grade-bounties are ever built, settlement will trigger when the grade is published and never on what it says: a payout identical at 9/10 and 1/10 is the only version that leaves the number worth reading.
