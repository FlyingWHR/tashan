The demand board

Request a grade — not a placement.

Other stores let you pay for a placement. Here you request a capability to be tracked and graded — the board is ranked by measured demand, and the grade you get is the one the evidence gives, whatever it says. You can buy the measurement; you can't buy the number.

Preview — illustrative data. The backing amounts, backer counts and “funded” states below are example figures, not live ones: no money has been pledged and nothing has been funded. This is what the demand board will look like once grade-bounties exist. “Back” does work — it opens a public GitHub issue registering demand — but it moves no money and buys no position.
The firewall. You fund the grade, never the score. A grade-bounty settles the moment the grade is published — and the payout is identical whether the capability scores 9/10 or 1/10. Money buys priority, depth, and access; it can never touch the number. Funder, grader, and scoring engine are separate.
